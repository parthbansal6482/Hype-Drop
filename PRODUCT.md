# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
High-end streetwear collectors seeking exclusivity, premium quality, and a raw editorial aesthetic.

## Product Purpose
To serve as an ultra-minimalist, high-end online luxury storefront for the apparel brand Hype-Drop, allowing users to discover drops, view premium products, and complete checkouts.

## Brand Personality
Bold, cold, highly confident, and minimal. Swiss-style typographic restraint.

## Anti-references
Typical e-commerce templates: no rounded corners (0px radii), no shadows, no gradients, no generic 'ghost-cards', and no marketing buzzwords (e.g. 'unleash', 'supercharge').

## Design Principles
1. Typographic Dominance: Swiss-inspired, high font-weight contrast, geometric clean layout.
2. Uncompromising Geometry: Sharp 0px corners, flat fills, structured lines, no decorative blur.
3. Editorial Restraint: Let high-contrast copy and curated imagery tell the product story.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
High WCAG contrast compliance (pure black on pure white), keyboard navigation compatibility, and prefers-reduced-motion alternatives for all animations.
